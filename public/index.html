How to play

  1. Join. One person hosts and shares the 4-letter code. Everyone enters it with a nickname. You need 3 to 8 players.
  2. One suspect per case. The suspect rotates, so everyone gets a turn. The suspect secretly did it or didn't, and knows their story.
  3. Detectives ask questions. Type in the box and press ENTER. The suspect must answer before the timer runs out. Use FOLLOW-UP to push a question to the front of the line.
  4. Hold SPACE to read your private cards. Only you can see them. Detectives can PIN a clue to the shared evidence board.
  5. Vote in secret. After deliberation, detectives vote GUILTY or INNOCENT (keys G and I). The majority decides, and a tie counts as innocent.
  6. Score. Detectives score for correct votes. The suspect scores for getting off. The player with the most points after all cases wins.

Power cards are one-time bonuses. Check your card on the right during the interrogation.
